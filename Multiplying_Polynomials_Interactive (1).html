🌎 Why does this matter?

🏡 Area: A garden with dimensions (x + 8) and (x + 3) has area x² + 11x + 24 square feet.

📦 Volume: Three variable dimensions can be multiplied to model a box.

💰 Business: Price × quantity can produce a polynomial revenue model.

✨ M — MODEL (I DO) 👨‍🏫

1️⃣ Monomial × Monomial

(3x²)(4x³)

Multiply coefficients: 3 × 4 = 12. Add exponents with the same base: x² × x³ = x⁵.

✅ 12x⁵

2️⃣ Monomial × Polynomial

−2x(3x² − 4x + 6)

Distribute −2x to all three terms:

−6x³ + 8x² − 12x

✅ −6x³ + 8x² − 12x

3️⃣ Binomial × Binomial (FOIL)

(x + 3)(x + 5)

🥇 First: x²   🌈 Outer: 5x
💎 Inner: 3x   🏁 Last: 15

x² + 5x + 3x + 15

✅ x² + 8x + 15

4️⃣ Binomial × Trinomial

(x + 2)(x² + 3x + 4)

x(x² + 3x + 4) + 2(x² + 3x + 4)

x³ + 3x² + 4x + 2x² + 6x + 8

✅ x³ + 5x² + 10x + 8

📦 The Box Method

Multiply (x + 2)(x + 3) by filling in every cell.

×x+3
xx²3x
+22x6

Combine: x² + 3x + 2x + 6

✅ x² + 5x + 6

💎 Special Products

🌟 Square of a sum: (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

Example: (x + 4)² = x² + 8x + 16

🌟 Square of a difference: (a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²

Example: (x − 5)² = x² − 10x + 25

🌟 Difference of squares: (a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²

Example: (x + 7)(x − 7) = x² − 49

🔍 A — ANALYZE

Compare (x + 3)² = x² + 6x + 9 with (x + 3)(x − 3) = x² − 9.

🤔 Why is there no x-term in the second product? Because +3x and −3x cancel!

⚠️ Common mistakes: forgetting to distribute to every term, multiplying exponents instead of adding them, losing negative signs, and forgetting the middle term when squaring a binomial.
